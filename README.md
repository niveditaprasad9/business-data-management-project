# business-data-management-project
# Optimizing Inventory Management and Profitability Analysis for a Retail Store

This project aims to provide data-driven recommendations to address inventory management and improve overall profitability of a retail store. The primary objectives are to optimize inventory levels, reduce stockouts and overstock situations, and improve overall business profitability through sales and inventory analysis.

##  Project Overview

- Conducted a comprehensive analysis of **21 weeks of Point-of-Sale (POS) data** collected from Astha RCM PUC.
- Analyzed product sales, inventory movement, and profitability across multiple product categories.
- Performed **Demand Fluctuation Analysis, Demand Forecasting, Inventory Optimization, and Product Profitability Analysis** to identify sales patterns and improve inventory decisions.
- Developed actionable recommendations for inventory planning, reorder policies, and product prioritization to support business decision-making.

##  Data Collection

The project involved collecting weekly Point-of-Sale (POS) data containing the following information:

- Product Name
- Opening Stock
- Quantity In
- Quantity Out (Sales)
- Closing Stock (Balance)
- Cost Price
- Selling Price
- Sales Amount
- Tax Rate

##  Analysis Methods

### 1. Demand Fluctuation Analysis
- Calculated the **Coefficient of Variation (CV%)** to measure demand variability.
- Classified products into:
  - Stable Demand
  - Moderate Fluctuation
  - Highly Fluctuating Demand

### 2. Demand Forecasting
- Applied a **3-week Simple Moving Average (SMA)** technique to forecast future product demand.

### 3. Inventory Optimization
- Calculated:
  - Safety Stock
  - Reorder Point (ROP)
  - Maximum Stock Threshold
- Performed **ABC Analysis** based on cumulative sales value to prioritize inventory management.

### 4. Product Profitability Analysis
- Calculated:
  - Total Profit
  - Total Sales Volume
- Compared products based on profit and sales volume using  heatmap visualizations.

##  Results and Recommendations

The analysis provided valuable business insights, including:

- Identified products with stable and fluctuating demand to improve inventory planning.
- Recommended dynamic reorder policies for products with moderate and highly variable demand.
- Prioritized **Category A** products for tighter inventory control due to their significant contribution to revenue.
- Identified high-profit and high-sales products for focused inventory management.
- Recommended reducing excess inventory of slow-moving products to minimize holding costs.
- Suggested regular monitoring of inventory levels and updating reorder points based on changing demand patterns.
